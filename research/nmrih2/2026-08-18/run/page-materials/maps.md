## 已核实事实

- [verified] Objective missions use large maps and linear tasks; Survival missions defend speaker systems through up to three waves.（[Welcome to No More Room in Hell 2!](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/11/welcome-to-no-more-room-in-hell-2/)）
- [verified] Survival gives preparation time and a supply drop, then five-minute speaker-defense waves; supply drops follow successful waves, helicopter extraction follows wave three, and early extraction opens after two failed waves.（[Update Notes: Armageddon (1.0)](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)）
- [verified] Flooded, Lighthouse and Night of the Living Dead are the three Armageddon Survival maps; Raven Rock is the sixth Objective map.（[Update Notes: Armageddon (1.0)](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/), [Road to 1.0: Release Date & Raven Rock](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/07/28/road-to-1-0-release-date-raven-rock/)）
