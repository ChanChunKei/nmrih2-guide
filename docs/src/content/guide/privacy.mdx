## Overview

NMRiH2 Guide is a static fan guide. We do not provide accounts, comments, purchases, or a private contact form on this site.

## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate page use and improve guides. Google Analytics may process information such as browser, device, approximate location, pages viewed, and interaction data according to Google’s own policies. Learn more in [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Public issue reports and external links

If you open a GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible under the repository’s settings. Guides also link to official game sites, Steam, and community sources; those services have their own privacy practices.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the site’s analytics, contact methods, or data practices materially change. The date at the bottom of the page shows its most recent review.
