description: Understand sights, rail accessories, and muzzle attachments in No More Room in Hell 2.

Weapon attachments help tune a firearm for a job; they do not replace positioning or squad coordination. The live game uses sight, rail, and muzzle attachment categories, with compatible slots varying by weapon.

## Sights

Use a sight when it helps you acquire targets cleanly at the ranges your team actually fights. Test its sight picture before committing to it: a clearer view is only useful if it remains comfortable during movement and close-range pressure.

## Rail accessories

Rail space is valuable. Add an accessory only when it solves a repeatable problem for your role, and avoid carrying an upgrade that does not change how you play.

## Muzzle attachments

Muzzle choices can affect how you approach engagements. Treat them as part of the squad’s wider plan: communicate before creating noise, and do not assume one attachment makes an unsafe route safe.

<div class="guide-callout">Attachment behavior can change in updates. Verify the in-game tooltip for exact compatibility and effects before making a loadout decision.</div>
