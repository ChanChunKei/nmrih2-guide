A good loadout starts with a role: dependable close defense, supporting teammates, carrying objective utility, or covering longer sight lines. Give each item a reason to be there.

## A practical checklist

### Cover one clear squad job

- Can you handle an unexpected close-range encounter?
- Do you have enough supplies to contribute without overloading your inventory?
- Does the team already cover the same utility?
- Will losing this setup hurt more than it helps this run?

## Build for adaptation

Keep one part of your plan flexible. The map, squad size, and loot you find should be allowed to change your decisions. A loadout is a starting point, not a promise to ignore better tools on the route.

## Armageddon 1.0 progression

Version 1.0 added Merits, earned through weekly Assignments, alongside Credits. It also introduced the Rescue Beacon: a high-end loadout item that can be bought with Credits or Merits and prevents the next loss a Responder would receive where permadeath is enabled. It occupies a loadout slot and is consumable, so treat it as protection with a real tradeoff rather than a permanent safety net. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Loadout budget

Give every slot one purpose. If you cannot say when an item is used, keep the slot flexible.

| Template | Starting priority | Tradeoff to accept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning run | Simple weapon handling, room for found supplies, no dependence on a rare plan. | Lower short-term specialization. |
| Team support | A clear close-defense answer plus room for medical or task utility. | Do not duplicate a teammate’s expensive kit without a reason. |
| High-risk Responder | A plan that still works when supplies are scarce; consider Rescue Beacon only if its slot cost fits the role. | Rescue Beacon is consumable and replaces another loadout option. |

Weekly Assignments can grant Credits and Merits; three assignments grant one Merit each and the weekly overarching goal grants three. Use that progression as a reason to set a sustainable role, not to take equipment you cannot replace. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)
