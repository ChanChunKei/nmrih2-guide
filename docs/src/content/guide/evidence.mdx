NMRiH2 Guide is an independent fan resource. This page shows the evidence records behind claims already published on the site, along with any version-sensitive claims that are waiting for a fresh review.

## How does NMRiH2 Guide verify a claim?

**Officially confirmed** means a reachable official announcement, update note, platform page, or current in-game description supports the statement. **Community-tested** means two independent, public player sources on different domains support the same reproducible conclusion for a compatible version.

One-source player observations can be retained on their relevant guide as **Community-reported**, but never as a fixed route, a best-in-slot conclusion, or a recommendation. Internal research candidates and source conflicts are not published here.

### When is evidence reviewed again?

Live events and similar volatile details are reviewed within seven days. Build-sensitive mechanics, maps, routes, and rankings are reviewed within 30 days. Durable facts are reviewed after relevant official patches or at least every 180 days.

## How to report a source problem

If a source is unavailable, a version has changed, or a statement needs correction, include the guide URL, the specific claim, the version or date you tested, and a direct source when you [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/ChanChunKei/nmrih2-guide/issues/new). See the full [editorial standards](/about/) for the site’s independence and correction policy.
