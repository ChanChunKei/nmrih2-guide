## Armageddon 1.0 map additions

Armageddon added Raven Rock as an Objective map and introduced the Survival mode maps Flooded, Lighthouse, and Night of the Living Dead. Survival challenges Responders to defend speakers across three waves; after a successful third wave, a helicopter extracts the team. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Use every map guide the same way

1. Learn the opening objective and the likely regroup point.
2. Mark a fallback route before the team is under pressure.
3. Decide who is carrying essential supplies.
4. Extract when the run has achieved its goal; do not turn success into a risky detour.

## Choose the correct plan before deploying

| Mode | First decision | Mid-match reset | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | Identify the current CRC task and its carrier/interactor. | Recount players and supplies after every loud objective beat. | Use final extraction after the task chain; early extract if the route collapses. |
| Survival | Select one speaker defense, cover lanes, and keep a rotation path open. | Spend the post-wave supply drop on the weak point before moving on. | Hold three waves for helicopter extraction; early extraction opens after two failed waves. |

Survival maps are **Flooded**, **Lighthouse**, and **Night of the Living Dead**. **Raven Rock** is the sixth Objective map. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/) · [Raven Rock announcement](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/07/28/road-to-1-0-release-date-raven-rock/)

### Before the ready check

- Name a regroup landmark and one player who calls the next task.
- Say which player carries task items and who watches reload/heal windows.
- Agree on the condition that ends optional looting: infection, low medical supplies, repeated downs, or a completed objective.
- Read the map page’s **1.0 changes** section. Fixed routes and loot spots are intentionally omitted until current walkthrough evidence can verify them.
