Skills are a long-term choice, so a good rule is simple: select for the way your squad survives, not for a hypothetical perfect build.

## Choose a clear purpose

### Pick one repeatable squad job

Prioritize a skill when it consistently improves one of these jobs: keeping the team supplied, handling pressure safely, reaching objectives, or recovering from mistakes. A smaller benefit that helps every run is often more useful than a situational pick.

## Current 1.0 skill pool

Armageddon 1.0 added Handgun Reloading, Rifle Reloading, and Shotgun Reloading. It also removed Averaging, Guts, and Teddy Bear. Build recommendations written before August 2026 may therefore describe a different skill pool.

## Pair skills with the team

Tell the squad what your selection enables. The value of a skill grows when teammates know when to rely on it and when to protect the player using it.

## Choose by job

| Squad job | Look for a skill that repeatedly helps… | Tell the squad… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sustain | treatment, inventory, or recovery | which supplies or recovery moments you can support |
| Pressure control | weapon handling, movement, or safe disengage | when you need cover to use that advantage |
| Objective support | task progress, carrying, or getting the team to the next stage | when the team should protect the interaction instead of looting |

Armageddon 1.0 added **Handgun Reloading**, **Rifle Reloading**, and **Shotgun Reloading**, while removing **Averaging**, **Guts**, and **Teddy Bear**. That means pre-1.0 build pages can describe a different pool. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

Do not publish or follow an effect list without checking the current in-game skill screen: names, requirements, and effects may change. The useful decision is not “what is best?” but “what job will this Responder perform for this squad?”
