Night of the Living Dead is an Armageddon 1.0 Survival map based on the classic farmhouse setting. The updated version expands the space with a guest house and barn.

## Use the property deliberately

### Set retreat lanes before each wave

Choose firing positions that leave teammates a route to retreat, and do not let a single player drift too far from the speaker defense. Between waves, redistribute ammunition and healing supplies before searching the property again.

## Extract on purpose

Complete three successful waves to trigger helicopter extraction. If two waves fail, early extraction opens; use that option when it protects a run that is no longer stable. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Choose the hold by what it gives up

| Area | Verified advantage | Team question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farmhouse | Three floors, traps that need fuel, and boards that can shape approaches. | Can everyone rotate without blocking the stairs? |
| Guest house | Open layout, exterior doors/windows, garage supplies, and a cellar. | Who watches the multiple exterior approaches? |
| Barn | Large interior and lofts that suit longer-range play, with fewer defensive options. | Can the squad cover pressure without relying on traps? |

These locations are official map descriptions. Pick the area that leaves the squad a retreat lane, then redistribute ammunition and medicine after every wave before searching again. [Official Night of the Living Dead overview](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/07/23/road-to-1-0-survival-night-of-the-living-dead/)

## Match the plan to the property

### Choose a purpose, then protect the rotation

The farmhouse, guest house, and barn each offer different confirmed tradeoffs, but none establishes an objectively best hold. Choose the area whose strengths support the current squad—controlled approaches, a shared line of sight, or room for longer-range coverage—then state a route everyone can use to rotate. The important part is not defending every entrance alone; it is moving together before an isolated player becomes the weak point. [Official Night of the Living Dead overview](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/07/23/road-to-1-0-survival-night-of-the-living-dead/)

| Between-wave question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Which approach nearly failed? | Use the supply reset to support that lane before adding private upgrades. |
| Can everyone use the fallback path? | Change the defense plan before the timer, not during the collapse. |
| Are supplies or players depleted? | Consider the early-extract option after two failed waves. |
| Has the third wave succeeded? | Regroup and move to helicopter extraction as a team. |
