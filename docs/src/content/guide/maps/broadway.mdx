title: Broadway Guide
description: Stay coordinated through Broadway’s high-pressure urban routes in No More Room in Hell 2.
---

Broadway demands calm communication. The map’s urban pressure makes it easy for one player’s rushed decision to pull the entire squad into a difficult encounter.

## Make space for the squad

Avoid blocking doorways and narrow passages. Let the team know before crossing a choke point, and give anyone who is reloading or using an item time to finish before pushing onward.

## Control the pace

The correct speed is the one that keeps every player informed. Slow down after a noisy engagement, take stock of supplies, and decide the next move together.

## Adapt without splitting

If the route changes, change it as a group. A short detour with everyone present is safer than a fast shortcut taken alone.
