Raven Rock is the Objective-map addition in Armageddon 1.0. It begins outside an underground military bunker in mountainous southern Pennsylvania, where Responders investigate the fate of emergency operations personnel.

## Run it as an objective map

### Protect the objective player

Keep the player handling the active objective protected, and use every quiet section to regroup and redistribute supplies. As with other Objective maps, do not spend the team’s recovery resources on an optional detour without agreeing on an exit plan.

## Conservative bunker protocol

### Keep transitions deliberate

Raven Rock starts outside an underground military bunker, so the squad should make a new regroup call whenever the environment or task phase changes. Before moving into the next transition, name the current objective player, the rear watcher, and the short fallback direction. This is more reliable than relying on a remembered route from an older build.

| Moment | Team action | Do not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A task updates | Read the live objective log and name the next carrier/interactor. | A video’s scenario order still applies. |
| The team enters a new section | Recount people, medical items, and ready ammunition. | Everyone is behind the lead player. |
| The objective finishes | Call extraction or one bounded detour. | A completed task makes the return harmless. |

## Failure and extraction boundary

If the squad loses its route call, has an infected or unsupported player, or cannot protect the next interaction, stop pushing and use the last readable space to reset. Early extraction is an intentional lower-reward outcome that can preserve a Responder; final extraction is for a team that can still protect the completed task chain.
