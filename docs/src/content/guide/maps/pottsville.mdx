---
title: Pottsville Guide
description: Prepare for a disciplined Pottsville run in No More Room in Hell 2.
---

Pottsville is an urban rescue route where information wins time. Keep callouts short, maintain line of sight with the squad, and use clear landmarks whenever the group changes direction.

## Route priorities

Move through one area at a time. Search only the spaces that support the current objective or resupply a teammate, then regroup before pushing forward.

## Team coordination

Crowded routes make separation costly. Assign someone to confirm the rear is moving, call out supplies before taking them, and avoid starting a fight while another player is still navigating the previous space.

## Leave with the team

Before extraction, count the players you expect to leave with. A safe exit is more valuable than a final, uncoordinated loot run.
