Lighthouse is an Armageddon 1.0 Survival map set around a beacon on a cliffside. A small neighborhood and abandoned construction contain useful tools, while the lighthouse itself creates tight spaces where the squad can quickly be trapped.

## Defend without blocking each other

### Leave room to rotate

Before a wave, agree on which player covers each narrow approach and keep a clear path for teammates who need to reload, heal, or rotate. The team should be able to fall back together rather than squeezing through the same doorway under pressure.

## Treat the beacon as the priority

The Survival objective is protecting speakers through successive waves. Loot only while it improves that defense, then return to the group before the timer begins. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Defense plan

The lighthouse has tight spaces and the cliffside limits retreat options. Before each wave, assign narrow approaches, preserve one route for players who must reload/heal, and agree where the group rotates if the first hold fails. A solo player chasing loot is less useful than a player who returns before the timer with a clear lane to defend.

Survival sends a helicopter after three successful waves and opens early extraction after two failed waves. [Official Survival rules](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)
