---
title: Lewiston Guide
description: A team-first Lewiston route guide for No More Room in Hell 2.
---

Lewiston is best approached with a flexible plan. Agree on the immediate task, protect whoever is solving it, and reserve enough supplies for the route back out.

## Keep the run readable

Use one voice for the next objective and one clear regroup call. If the team loses track of the plan, stop in a defensible position, restate it, and move together again.

## Loot without losing tempo

Give each player a short, visible search area. If the needed item is not there, leave rather than turning a single check into a prolonged split.

## Extraction

When the group has the objective and enough resources to leave, treat extraction as the next objective—not a reward to delay.
