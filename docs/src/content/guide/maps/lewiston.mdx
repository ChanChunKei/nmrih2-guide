Lewiston is best approached with a flexible plan. Agree on the immediate task, protect whoever is solving it, and reserve enough supplies for the route back out.

## Keep the run readable

### Reset the callout plan

Use one voice for the next objective and one clear regroup call. If the team loses track of the plan, stop in a defensible position, restate it, and move together again.

## Loot without losing tempo

Give each player a short, visible search area. If the needed item is not there, leave rather than turning a single check into a prolonged split.

## Extraction

When the group has the objective and enough resources to leave, treat extraction as the next objective—not a reward to delay.

Armageddon adjusted the train tunnel puzzle, added a Bio-Scanner requirement for the chapel during the hard-drive route, and requires the finale gates to be powered before opening. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## 1.0 objective checklist

- On the hard-drive route, confirm who has the Bio-Scanner before committing to the chapel.
- Do not call the finale “open” until the squad has confirmed its gates are powered.
- During the train-tunnel sequence, keep one player on the active task and the rest on the route and rear; do not turn a puzzle pause into a split-loot break.

These are official 1.0 changes, not a promise of a fixed route. Check the live objective log when a scenario differs. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)
