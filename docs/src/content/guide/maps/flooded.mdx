Flooded is a Survival map added in Armageddon 1.0. Its industrial complex in rural Pennsylvania includes multi-floor buildings, cargo containers, abandoned machinery, and groundwater that makes confirming kills and sharing loot more difficult.

## Hold the speaker together

### Assign every approach

Survival gives the squad time to loot and prepare around speakers before each wave. Prioritize a defensible position, call out who is watching each approach, and save supply drops for the team’s weak point rather than individual upgrades.

## Plan for three waves

Each successful wave gives the squad a short reset and another supply drop. After the third success, the helicopter arrives for extraction; after two failed waves, early extraction becomes available. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Wave checklist

| Before the timer | During the wave | After the wave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pick one dry, readable resupply point and assign approach lanes. | Keep a rotation lane open; call reloads and use traps/defenses only when pressure threatens the speaker. | Spend the new supply drop on the weakest lane, then decide whether to move as one group. |

Flooded's storm-hit industrial ground makes kill confirmation and loot sharing harder. Do not let water-covered space turn into a silent split: regroup before moving between points of interest. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Between-wave recovery plan

### Fix the weakest lane first

Flooded is one of the three Armageddon 1.0 Survival maps. Its official description emphasizes multi-floor industrial spaces, containers, machinery, and groundwater; it does not establish a single best defense location. Use the preparation phase to choose readable lanes and one rotation direction, then use the post-wave supply drop to solve the part of the defense that nearly failed. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

| Wave state | Team response |
| --- | --- |
| Before the speaker starts | Confirm approach calls, a rotation route, and who carries recovery items. |
| A lane is overwhelmed | Call the rotation; do not have each player choose a different retreat. |
| A wave succeeds | Refill the weakest shared need before exploring further. |
| Two waves fail | Discuss early extraction instead of treating wave three as mandatory. |
