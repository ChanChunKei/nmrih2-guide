title: Power Plant Guide
description: Plan a controlled Power Plant run in No More Room in Hell 2.
---

Power Plant rewards preparation. The opening and objective path can vary, so use landmarks and squad communication rather than relying on a single memorized route.

## Before moving out

Set a regroup point, decide who watches the rear, and avoid splitting the squad just to search every side room. Keep the team ready to change direction when the objective asks for it.

## Objective discipline

When an objective item is found, announce it immediately. Let the group protect the player carrying it, clear the next short section together, and only then resume looting.

## 1.0 update

Armageddon 1.0 removed Power Plant’s Bio-Scanner initial objective scenario and adjusted stash-room loot to scale better with player counts at each point of interest. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Extraction mindset

As soon as the objective is complete, make an explicit choice: extract now or take one limited, agreed detour. Unplanned wandering is where organized runs usually fall apart.

## 1.0 field checklist

Power Plant no longer uses the Bio-Scanner as an initial objective scenario, and stash-room loot now scales with player count at each point of interest. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

1. Read the active scenario before leaving spawn; older route videos can start from a different premise.
2. When a task item appears, make its carrier visible and stop treating side rooms as the priority.
3. At every task completion, call whether the squad is continuing to the next objective or beginning extraction.
