Beaulieu Hospital concentrates the team into closer spaces, so positioning and information matter. Move with enough room to react and avoid trapping teammates behind you.

## Before entering a new area

### Set an entry order

Confirm where the team is going next, which player is carrying important supplies, and where the group will fall back if the room is not safe to hold.

## Search deliberately

Hospital interiors reward concise checks. Open a space with teammates covering different angles, take what supports the run, and avoid lingering after the group has what it needs.

## Keep objectives visible

Call out every objective update and wait for the group before committing to the next stage. A single player progressing alone can leave the rest of the team without a clear route or support.

## 1.0 update

The release notes moved back ventilation parts that were close to the ventilation van and fixed multiple exploitable areas. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Room-entry protocol

- Name the next room and the fallback door before opening it.
- Keep the task carrier behind the first two players until the space is readable.
- When ventilation items are found, announce the carrier and immediately decide whether the team is returning or continuing.

The ventilation-part placement and exploit fixes changed in 1.0; do not rely on old shortcut or item-location clips. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

## Close-quarters reset rule

### Protect the carrier, not just the room

Armageddon 1.0 moved ventilation parts away from the van and corrected several exploitable areas. That makes older item-location clips and shortcuts unreliable. When a ventilation item is found, name its carrier, establish the immediate return or next-task plan, and keep the carrier behind the first two players until the next space is clear. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

| Moment | Team call |
| --- | --- |
| Before an entry | Name the next space and the fallback direction. |
| A task item appears | Announce the carrier and stop speculative searches. |
| A room becomes unreadable | Fall back together; do not have players squeeze past each other. |
| The task is done | Count medical supplies and choose extraction or one named continuation. |
