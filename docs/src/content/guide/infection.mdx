Infection turns a personal problem into a squad decision. The best response is early communication: tell teammates what happened, state what supplies you have, and agree whether to continue or change the route.

## Make a team plan

- Keep treatment-related supplies visible to the group rather than silently carrying them.
- Call out a risky encounter before it becomes a surprise for the next player.
- Do not separate from support when the team may need to make a quick decision.

## Know what is current

Official material describes infection as a core game mechanic. It identifies pills and a gene therapy cure as treatment options before an infected Responder dies and rises against their allies. [Official game overview](https://www.nmrih2.com/)
