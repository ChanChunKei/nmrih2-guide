No More Room in Hell 2 rewards a squad that stays organized more than one player trying to do everything. Treat your first runs as practice in communication, route choice, and knowing when to leave.

## Before you deploy

- Pick a role your team is missing instead of duplicating the same gear.
- Agree on a simple regroup call before anyone moves away from the spawn.
- Keep enough free inventory space for items that solve the next objective or keep a teammate alive.

## The core loop

1. Regroup and identify the first safe direction.
2. Loot with a purpose; do not split the party for low-value detours.
3. Call out enemies, supplies, locked paths, and objective progress.
4. Reassess after every major fight: continue, regroup, or extract.

## Good squad habits

Move at the pace of the least-ready teammate. Close doors behind the group when that buys time, but do not assume a closed door is permanent safety. Save ammunition and healing supplies for the moments when the team cannot avoid a fight.
