No More Room in Hell 2 rewards a squad that stays organized more than one player trying to do everything. Treat your first runs as practice in communication, route choice, and knowing when to leave.

## Before you deploy

### Agree on the first minute

- Pick a role your team is missing instead of duplicating the same gear.
- Agree on a simple regroup call before anyone moves away from the spawn.
- Keep enough free inventory space for items that solve the next objective or keep a teammate alive.

## The core loop

1. Regroup and identify the first safe direction.
2. Loot with a purpose; do not split the party for low-value detours.
3. Call out enemies, supplies, locked paths, and objective progress.
4. Reassess after every major fight: continue, regroup, or extract.

## Good squad habits

Move at the pace of the least-ready teammate. Close doors behind the group when that buys time, but do not assume a closed door is permanent safety. Save ammunition and healing supplies for the moments when the team cannot avoid a fight.

## First-match checklist

> **Version scope:** Armageddon 1.0, checked August 18, 2026. Use the official Tutorial or Solo Mode to learn maps without normal progression or permadeath risk. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

| Moment | Do this | Do not do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn | Read the mode, name a regroup landmark, and give one player the route call. | Split into side rooms before everyone knows the first task. |
| Loot | Reserve space for task items, medicine, and ammunition your squad can use. | Fill every slot with upgrades that do not solve the next objective. |
| Objective | Announce the carrier/interactor; have teammates cover before the interaction begins. | Start a holdout or carry objective alone. |
| After a fight | Count players, medical items, and the next safe move before advancing. | Reload, heal, or sort inventory in the open. |
| Extraction | Decide “leave now” or one named detour, then move as a group. | Turn a finished run into unplanned looting. |

Objective missions use large maps and staged CRC tasks; Survival uses speaker-defense waves. Early extraction preserves a Responder but pays less and gives no character progression, while final extraction awards the team-supply result. [Official launch overview](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/11/welcome-to-no-more-room-in-hell-2/)

## Recovery rule

When a run becomes unreadable, stop moving forward. Regroup in the safest available space, state the current objective, the infected/low-supply players, and the extraction option. A clear reset is more valuable than a fast push with no support.
