No More Room in Hell 2 rewards a squad that stays organized more than one player trying to do everything. Treat your first runs as practice in communication, route choice, and knowing when to leave.

## Before you deploy

### Agree on the first minute

- Pick a role your team is missing instead of duplicating the same gear.
- Agree on a simple regroup call before anyone moves away from the spawn.
- Keep enough free inventory space for items that solve the next objective or keep a teammate alive.

## The core loop

1. Regroup and identify the first safe direction.
2. Loot with a purpose; do not split the party for low-value detours.
3. Call out enemies, supplies, locked paths, and objective progress.
4. Reassess after every major fight: continue, regroup, or extract.

## Good squad habits

Move at the pace of the least-ready teammate. Close doors behind the group when that buys time, but do not assume a closed door is permanent safety. Save ammunition and healing supplies for the moments when the team cannot avoid a fight.

## First-match checklist

> **Version scope:** Armageddon 1.0, checked August 18, 2026. Use the official Tutorial or Solo Mode to learn maps without normal progression or permadeath risk. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)

| Moment | Do this | Do not do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn | Read the mode, name a regroup landmark, and give one player the route call. | Split into side rooms before everyone knows the first task. |
| Loot | Reserve space for task items, medicine, and ammunition your squad can use. | Fill every slot with upgrades that do not solve the next objective. |
| Objective | Announce the carrier/interactor; have teammates cover before the interaction begins. | Start a holdout or carry objective alone. |
| After a fight | Count players, medical items, and the next safe move before advancing. | Reload, heal, or sort inventory in the open. |
| Extraction | Decide “leave now” or one named detour, then move as a group. | Turn a finished run into unplanned looting. |

Objective missions use large maps and staged CRC tasks; Survival uses speaker-defense waves. Early extraction preserves a Responder but pays less and gives no character progression, while final extraction awards the team-supply result. [Official launch overview](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/11/welcome-to-no-more-room-in-hell-2/)

## Recovery rule

When a run becomes unreadable, stop moving forward. Regroup in the safest available space, state the current objective, the infected/low-supply players, and the extraction option. A clear reset is more valuable than a fast push with no support.

## A complete first-run routine

### Turn the first minute into a shared plan

Before the first door or road crossing, say the mode, the immediate objective, and the regroup call aloud. Give one player the next-route call and another player responsibility for noticing whether the rear is still present. These are temporary jobs, not permanent classes: the point is to make sure every question has an owner while the squad is still calm.

When the first useful item appears, ask a practical question instead of automatically taking it: *does this help the next task, a teammate, or the exit?* If the answer is no, leave the inventory space open. That one habit makes later objective items and emergency supplies easier to carry.

### Advance in short, reviewable stages

Treat an Objective deployment as a chain of small commitments. Clear a short section, confirm the next task, and move only once everyone can name the next stop. In Survival, use the preparation window to decide who covers each approach and where the group rotates if pressure breaks the first position. The exact layout changes, but the team conversation does not.

| Situation | Squad call | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A teammate finds a task item | Name the carrier and clear their next few steps. | The item does not become a solo errand. |
| Two players want to search | Give each a visible, short area and a return time. | The group does not silently become two squads. |
| A fight creates confusion | Fall back, count players, reload and restate the task. | You trade a few seconds for a readable run. |
| The objective is complete | Choose extraction or one named detour. | “One more room” cannot become an unplanned wipe. |

### Make extraction an active decision

Early extraction is not a failure state; it is a lower-reward option that preserves a Responder when continuing no longer has a stable plan. Final extraction is worth pursuing only when the squad still has enough people, supplies, and a shared route to finish. The official launch overview distinguishes the lower early-extract reward from the final-extract team-supply result, so do not promise new players that every safe exit pays the same. [Official launch overview](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/11/welcome-to-no-more-room-in-hell-2/)

## Common first-run mistakes

### Treating loot as the objective

Useful supplies matter, but a long search with no return plan costs time, attention, and squad cohesion. Stop looting when the team has the item needed for the next stage, a player is injured or infected, or the group is already ready to extract.

### Hiding a problem until it is urgent

Call low ammunition, injury, infection, or a lost route early. Teammates can cover a reload or change direction only while there is room to act. See the [Infection guide](/infection/) for the treatment and extraction decision boundary.

### Building around untested gear

The Tutorial and Solo Mode added in Armageddon 1.0 are the right place to learn inventory flow, combat, and objectives without normal progression or permadeath risk. Test controls and new weapons there; bring a simple, flexible plan to a live match. [Official 1.0 notes](https://www.nmrih2.com/2026/08/10/armageddon-1-0-update-notes/)
